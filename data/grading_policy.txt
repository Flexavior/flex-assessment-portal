# Grading policy (live-read before scoring — not a mark scheme)

ANSWER FORMAT:
Students may use their own section numbers (e.g. 3.1, 3.2), topic titles, or chapter
headings. They are NOT required to label answers as Q1, Q2, etc.
Match assignment requirements to submission content by TOPIC MEANING, not label.

COURSE MATERIAL USE:
- Slides (data/slides/): primary facts, terminology, expected diagrams.
- Textbook (data/textbook/): deeper reasoning and cohesion when slides are brief.
Do not contradict slides; use the book to extend or support.

DIAGRAMS AND FIGURES:
If the student embeds figures with captions or explanatory prose nearby, score the
related assignment topic — do not treat as "no text" when the document has substantial
written content elsewhere.

SCORING AUTHORITY:
- Rubric = marks and bands.
- Assignment brief = required topics.
- This file = retrieval and matching rules only.
